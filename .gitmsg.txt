fix(services): add start-services bat to launch both services without the watchdog

The ai-live-watchdog scheduled task is disabled on purpose now, so when
voice_server(8770) or vision.py dies nothing brings it back. Symptoms:
- camera panel turns grey (/cam.mjpg has no frames, vision/latest.jpg stale)
- HUD brightness / motion / look / emotion all freeze
- page looks like "Live2D and camera failed to load"

This bat only starts the services; it does not touch the scheduled task.
